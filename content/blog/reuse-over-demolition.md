The most sustainable building is the one that already exists. It already holds its embodied carbon, its history and, usually, a good deal of character.

## Start with what is there

A retrofit begins with understanding: the structure, the grid, the way the floor plates relate to the street. A building that seems awkward is often only unfamiliar, and its constraints can become the organising idea of the new scheme.

## Why it is hard

Reuse is not simply the cheaper or greener choice by default. It asks more of the design team early on, and the unknowns behind the plaster are real.

- **Surveys** need to happen before the concept, not after it.
- **Contingency** has to be honest about what cannot yet be seen.
- **Clients** need the story told in terms of value, not only carbon.

## Looking ahead

I would like to work on projects where the first question is "what can we keep?" rather than "what can we build?". The best answers I have seen start there.
